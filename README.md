[h1]罗马复兴（Roma Renascens）[/h1]

[h2]关于本 Mod[/h2]

这是一个为《维多利亚 3》制作的「希腊 → 拜占庭 → 罗马帝国」线性复兴剧本。

你将扮演希腊，在「伟大理想」的指引下点燃罗马的火种，从奥斯曼手中夺回故土，重建东罗马帝国（拜占庭）；随后一步步收复整个地中海世界的罗马疆域，最终宣布重建罗马，以 SPQR 之名再度君临天下。

[b]这是一个以叙事与爽感为主的 mod。[/b] 难度被刻意设计得很低，只要按部就班地推进议程，基本就是一路平推的体验——让玩家专注于重铸罗马荣光，而不是陷入数值挣扎。

[h2]主要玩法[/h2]

整体流程被拆成一系列国家议程（日志条目），按时间推进：

[list]
[*][b]希腊觉醒[/b] — 科技启蒙与文化复兴，提供创新力与税收加成。
[*][b]罗马火种[/b] — 点燃后十年内军团战力飙升，并获得奥斯曼治下故土的宣称。
[*][b]重建东罗马[/b] — 掌握全部奥斯曼故土后成立拜占庭，军团从「火种」蜕变为「纪律严明的职业军队」。
[*][b]重建罗马[/b] — 宣布后变为持续议程，获得「重建中的罗马」增益；随着奥古斯都议程逐次收复伊比利亚、多瑙河、高卢、不列颠，该增益会越来越强；当全部罗马疆域都归于鹰旗之下，升级为终极永久增益「永恒罗马」。
[*][b]奥古斯都[/b] — 按顺序宣称并征服意大利、伊比利亚、多瑙河、高卢、不列颠，最终加冕奥古斯都。另有「吞并阿拉伯半岛」按钮，达到列强后可直接整合。
[*][b]稳固行省[/b] — 把新征服的领土彻底纳入帝国统治，消除动乱。
[*][b]元老院[/b] — 通过三项罗马特色法律，获得「元老院的智慧」加成。
[/list]

[h2]主要改动[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]自定义罗马帝国标签 SPQR[/b]（区别于原版的罗马尼亚 ROM），含罗马鹰旗旗帜与罗马紫地图色。
[*][b]罗马文化、罗马多神教（Religio Romana）[/b]，重建罗马时主流文化与国教一并罗马化。
[*][b]罗马化利益集团名称与理念[/b]：军队→罗马军团、地主→元老院贵族、教廷→祭司团、实业家→骑士阶层等。
[*][b]数部罗马特色法律[/b]：罗马混合共和制、三级权力分配、万民法等。
[*][b]一系列永久 / 阶段性增益[/b]：重建中的罗马（四级递进）、条条大路通罗马、永恒罗马、元老院的智慧、万城之城、罗马和平等。
[*][b]地图与州定义调整[/b]，使罗马疆域覆盖完整的地中海世界。
[/list]

[h2]难度说明[/h2]

本 mod 定位是「罗马复兴爽文」：

[list]
[*]各种军事、经济、外交、行政增益叠加后，基本可以平推世界。
[*]恶名获取被大幅压低，外交博弈行动力充裕，征服体验顺滑。
[*]部分按钮可直接吞并领土（如爱琴海明珠、吞并阿拉伯半岛），省去繁琐战争。
[/list]

如果你在找严肃的平衡挑战，本 mod 暂不适合；它更适合想无压力体验「从希腊到罗马」成长弧线的玩家。

[h2]后续计划[/h2]

本 mod 仍在持续开发中，未来计划：

[list]
[*][b]难度平衡[/b]：目前偏爽，后续会逐步调整数值，让不同阶段有不同挑战。
[*][b]利益集团理念与法律偏好[/b]：丰富各 IG 的罗马化理念、诉求与对法律的态度，让内政更有层次。
[/list]

[h1]Roma Renascens[/h1]

[h2]About the Mod[/h2]

This is a linear "Greece → Byzantium → Roman Empire" restoration scenario for [i]Victoria 3[/i].

You will play as Greece, kindle the Roman spark under the Megali Idea, reclaim your ancestral lands from the Ottomans, and restore the Eastern Roman Empire (Byzantium); then step by step recover the full Roman expanse across the Mediterranean, finally declaring the restoration of Rome and reigning once more under the name SPQR.

[b]This is a narrative- and power-fantasy-oriented mod.[/b] The difficulty is deliberately kept low. Follow the journal entries in order and you will mostly steamroll everything — the focus is on forging Rome's glory, not wrestling with numbers.

[h2]Main Gameplay[/h2]

The whole arc is structured as a chain of journal entries progressing over time:

[list]
[*][b]Greek Awakening[/b] — an intellectual and cultural renaissance, granting innovation and tax boosts.
[*][b]Roman Spark[/b] — when lit, your legions fight at peak strength for a decade and you gain claims on the ancestral lands under Ottoman rule.
[*][b]Restore the Eastern Roman Empire[/b] — once you hold all former Ottoman Roman lands, form Byzantium, and the spark is tempered into a "Disciplined Professional Army".
[*][b]Declare Rome Reborn[/b] — becomes an ongoing journal after clicking, granting the "Rome Under Rebuilding" modifier; it grows stronger as you recover Hispania, the Danube, Gaul, and Britannia through the Augustan chain; once every Roman land flies the eagle standard, it upgrades into the ultimate permanent buff, "Eternal Rome".
[*][b]Augustus[/b] — claim and conquer Italy, Hispania, the Danube, Gaul, and Britannia in order, then be crowned Augustus. There is also an "Annex the Arabian Peninsula" button that directly incorporates the region once you are a Great Power.
[*][b]Secure the Provinces[/b] — fully integrate newly conquered lands and wipe out turmoil.
[*][b]The Senate[/b] — pass three Roman-themed laws to receive the "Wisdom of the Senate" buff.
[/list]

[h2]Key Changes[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]A custom Roman Empire tag SPQR[/b] (distinct from vanilla Romania ROM), with an SPQR eagle flag and Roman purple map color.
[*][b]Roman culture and Religio Romana[/b] — upon declaring Rome reborn, your primary culture and state religion are Romanized.
[*][b]Romanized interest group names and ideologies[/b]: Armed Forces → Roman Legions, Landowners → Senatorial Patricians, Devout → Pontifices, Industrialists → Equestrians, and more.
[*][b]Several Roman-themed laws[/b]: Roman Mixed Republic, Three-Tier Distribution of Power, Jus Gentium, etc.
[*][b]A set of permanent / stage modifiers[/b]: Rome Under Rebuilding (4 escalating tiers), All Roads Lead to Rome, Eternal Rome, Wisdom of the Senate, Eternal City, Pax Romana, etc.
[*][b]Map and state-region adjustments[/b] so the Roman expanse covers the full Mediterranean world.
[/list]

[h2]Difficulty[/h2]

This mod is meant as a "Roman restoration power fantasy":

[list]
[*]The stacked military, economic, diplomatic, and bureaucratic buffs let you basically conquer the world.
[*]Infamy gain is heavily reduced and diplomatic maneuvers are plentiful, making expansion smooth.
[*]Some buttons annex land directly (e.g. Pearl of the Aegean, Annex Arabia), skipping tedious wars.
[/list]

If you are looking for a serious balance challenge, this mod is not for you yet; it is aimed at players who want a stress-free ride through the Greece-to-Rome arc.
