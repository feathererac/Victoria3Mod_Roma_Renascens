[h1]罗马复兴（Roma Renascens）[/h1]

[h2]关于本 Mod[/h2]

这是一个为《维多利亚 3》制作的「希腊 → 拜占庭 → 罗马帝国」线性复兴剧本。
本mod高度依赖原版的希腊国家议程，需要先完成原版的希腊伟大理想国家议程成立拜占庭之后才能变身罗马。优化了原版宣称拜占庭事件的触发逻辑，确保只要拥有希腊的正常版图即可触发拜占庭的宣称（需要手动点宣称拜占庭）
（先研究”民族主义“，解锁原版伟大理想国家议程）
（开局日志里的决议可以直接吞并伊奥尼亚和克里特群岛）

你将扮演希腊，在「伟大理想」的指引下点燃罗马的火种，从奥斯曼手中夺回故土，重建东罗马帝国（拜占庭）；随后一步步收复整个地中海世界的罗马疆域，最终宣布重建罗马，以 SPQR 之名再度君临天下。

[b]这是一个以叙事与爽感为主的 mod。[/b] 难度被刻意设计得很低，只要按部就班地推进议程，基本就是一路平推的体验——让玩家专注于重铸罗马荣光。

[h2]主要玩法[/h2]

流程以国家议程形式推进：

[list]
[*][b]希腊觉醒[/b] — 科技启蒙与文化复兴。
[*][b]罗马火种[/b] — 军团战力飙升，获得奥斯曼故土宣称。
[*][b]重建东罗马[/b] — 收复奥斯曼故土后成立拜占庭。
[*][b]重建罗马[/b] — 宣布变身罗马，获得「重建中的罗马」增益，随征服逐步增强。
[*][b]奥古斯都[/b] — 依次吞并埃及/北非并征服意大利→伊比利亚→多瑙河→高卢→不列颠，最终加冕并升级为「永恒罗马」。
[*][b]稳固行省 / 元老院[/b] — 整合领土、推行罗马特色法律。
[/list]

[h2]主要改动[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]SPQR 帝国[/b]：罗马鹰旗旗帜与地图色。
[*][b]罗马文化、罗马多神教[/b]：重建时主流文化与国教罗马化。
[*][b]利益集团罗马化[/b]：军团、元老院、祭司团、骑士阶层等。
[*][b]罗马特色法律[/b]：混合共和制、三级权力分配、万民法。
[*][b]多级增益体系[/b]：重建中的罗马（四级）→ 永恒罗马。
[/list]

[h2]难度说明[/h2]

本 mod 定位是「罗马复兴爽文」：增益叠加后基本可平推世界，恶名被压低，部分按钮可直接吞并领土，专为无压力体验「从希腊到罗马」成长弧线设计。

[h2]后续计划[/h2]

难度数值平衡与利益集团理念、法律偏好的进一步丰富。

[h1]Roma Renascens[/h1]

[h2]About this Mod[/h2]

This is a linear revival scenario for Victoria 3: Greece → Byzantine Empire → Roman Empire.
The mod relies heavily on the vanilla Greek national agenda: you must first complete the vanilla "Great Idea" agenda to form the Byzantine Empire before transforming into Rome. The vanilla event logic for claiming Byzantium has been optimized so that holding Greece's regular territory is enough to trigger the Byzantine claim (the claim must be pressed manually).
(Research "Nationalism" first to unlock the vanilla Great Idea national agenda.)
(The decisions in your starting journal can directly annex the Ionian Islands and Crete.)

You play as Greece. Guided by the "Great Idea," you ignite the spark of Rome, reclaim your homeland from the Ottomans, and rebuild the Eastern Roman Empire (Byzantium). Then, step by step, you reconquer Rome's Mediterranean territories and finally declare the rebuilding of Rome, ruling the world once more under the name of SPQR.

[b]This is a narrative-driven, power-fantasy mod.[/b] Difficulty is deliberately set very low — as long as you follow the agenda step by step, it is essentially a smooth conquest experience, letting the player focus on restoring Rome's glory.

[h2]Core Gameplay[/h2]

The flow is driven through national agendas (journal entries):

[list]
[*][b]Greek Awakening[/b] — Technological enlightenment and cultural revival.
[*][b]Roman Spark[/b] — Legion combat power soars; gain claims on Ottoman lands.
[*][b]Rebuild Eastern Rome[/b] — Form Byzantium after reclaiming Ottoman territories.
[*][b]Rebuild Rome[/b] — Declare to transform into Rome and gain the "Rome Reborn" bonus, which strengthens with each conquest.
[*][b]Augustus[/b] — Annex Egypt/North Africa and conquer Italia → Iberia → Danube → Gaul → Britannia in sequence, then be coronated and upgrade to "Imperium Aeternum."
[*][b]Secure Provinces / Senate[/b] — Integrate territories and enact Rome-themed laws.
[/list]

[h2]Key Features[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]SPQR Empire[/b]: Roman eagle flag and map color.
[*][b]Roman culture & Roman polytheism[/b]: Primary culture and state religion are Romanized on rebuilding.
[*][b]Romanized interest groups[/b]: Legions, Senate, College of Pontiffs, Equestrians, etc.
[*][b]Rome-themed laws[/b]: Mixed Republic, Three-Tier Power Distribution, Jus Gentium.
[*][b]Tiered bonus system[/b]: Rome Reborn (four tiers) → Imperium Aeternum.
[/list]

[h2]Difficulty[/h2]

This mod is a "Roman revival power fantasy": stacked bonuses let you steamroll the world, infamy is heavily reduced, and some buttons directly annex territories. It is designed for a stress-free experience of Greece-to-Rome growth.

[h2]Roadmap[/h2]

Further difficulty balancing, and richer interest-group ideologies and law preferences.

Thank you for playing — may the eagle banner never fall.
